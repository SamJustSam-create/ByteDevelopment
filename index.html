01 · About

Locally based. Globally capable.

Byte Development is a one-person software studio ran out of Bendigo, Victoria. I work with small businesses, agencies, and growing teams to build the software they need - without the bloat, retainers, or jargon.

Most of my clients have been burned before by the shear overpriced agencies or unfinished freelance jobs. My focus is the opposite: small scopes, honest timelines, and code that someone else can actually maintain after I'm gone.
